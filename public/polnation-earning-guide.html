Module 1 — USDC Dividends
Earn daily rewards based on your USDC balance tier

The foundation of Polnation's earning system: simply hold USDC in your wallet and receive daily rewards. The platform takes daily snapshots of all user balances on-chain and distributes rewards proportional to your balance tier — no staking, no lock-up, no token approvals required.

💰 How it works: Every 24 hours, the platform reads your wallet's USDC balance via the ERC-20 balanceOf() function. Based on your balance tier, you're assigned a reward allocation. Rewards are distributed via Merkle Tree and claimable immediately.

⚠️ Key principle: Your USDC never leaves your wallet. The platform only reads your balance via the public balanceOf() function — a read-only operation that requires zero permissions. You can withdraw or transfer your USDC at any time without penalty.

Why This Model is Safe

  • Non-custodial: Your USDC stays in your wallet 100% of the time
  • No approvals: Platform never calls approve() or transferFrom()
  • Zero lock-up: Use your USDC for other DeFi activities anytime
  • On-chain proof: All balance reads are public on Polygon blockchain

Module 2 — Referral Rewards
Earn commissions from your downline up to 6 levels deep

Every time a member in your downline receives a profit distribution from the platform's staking & advertising revenue, you automatically receive a percentage commission — passed up through the referral chain with no manual action required.

💡 How it works: Commissions are calculated at the moment of each airdrop distribution cycle. The platform reads the on-chain referral tree and credits all uplines simultaneously in a single Merkle-verified batch.

What Counts as "Team Volume"?

Team volume is calculated from the total USDC held on-chain by members in your Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 downline. As your team grows and holds more USDC, your volume increases — pushing you toward the next level threshold.

Momentum Multiplier

To reward active team builders, Polnation applies a Momentum Multiplier to daily income. Members who regularly bring in new referrals maintain a higher multiplier (up to 5.0×). Inactivity causes gradual decay.

Effective Daily = Base Daily × Momentum (max 5.0×)
