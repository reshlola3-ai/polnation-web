1. What is Polnation?

Polnation is a crypto soft staking platform built on the Polygon blockchain. It allows users to earn daily USDC rewards simply by holding USDC in their own wallet — without ever locking or transferring their tokens.

🔑 Key Principles

  • Non-custodial — Your USDC stays in YOUR wallet at all times
  • No lock-up period — Withdraw your USDC anytime, no penalties
  • Daily earnings — Rewards distributed every 24 hours
  • On Polygon — Low gas fees, fast transactions

2. How It Works

1
Connect Wallet — Connect your MetaMask or Trust Wallet to Polnation
2
Hold USDC — Keep USDC (Polygon network) in your wallet
3
Sign Permit — One-time gasless signature to authorize reward distribution (your funds are NOT transferred)
4
Earn Daily — Receive USDC rewards directly to your wallet every 24 hours

💡 Important: The Permit signature only authorizes Polnation to read your balance and distribute rewards to you. You retain full control of your funds and can withdraw at any time.

5. Momentum Multiplier (5x Bonus)

The Momentum Multiplier is a gamification system that rewards active promoters by multiplying their daily community earnings up to 5x.

🚀 How It Works

All users start with a 5.0x multiplier. If you stay active by referring new staked members, your multiplier stays at 5x. If you stop promoting, the multiplier gradually decays.

Daily Earnings = Base Earning × Momentum Multiplier

Multiplier Tiers

Recent Staked Referrals Multiplier
4+ referrals5.0x
3 referrals4.0x
2 referrals3.0x
1 referral2.0x
0 referrals (inactive)1.0x (base)

Decay Rules

  • Without new referrals, the multiplier drops by 1.0x every 3 days
  • Minimum is 1.0x (you never lose your base earnings)
  • Instant recovery: Make a new staked referral and your multiplier jumps back up immediately

Example: Silver level ($100 pool, 1.0% rate) = $1/day base. With 5x momentum = $5/day!
